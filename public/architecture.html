Daromadchi — Multi-Marketplace Seller Dashboard
Syncs orders, inventory, and stock across Uzum Market and Yandex Market. Web dashboard + Chrome Extension for seller analytics. Marketplace integrations are read-only by default; the one exception is the opt-in, per-shop Stock-sync mode, which writes only the stock quantity (ostatok) through a single audited writer after the seller consents. Auth via NextAuth (Auth.js) v5 — Credentials + bcrypt, optional Google OAuth, JWT sessions, email verification. Google Analytics 4 is consent-gated. Self-hosted on a Hetzner VPS (Finland / Helsinki).
